# Nikkei Volatility Note
Date: 2025-11-18

Nikkei volatility recently rose due to a combination of JPY moves, policy-rate expectation repricing, and global risk-on/risk-off rotation.
When USDJPY swings and overseas semiconductor demand expectations shift, Japanese equities can see larger intraday dispersion.
For risk control, monitor liquidity depth, futures basis, and event windows around central-bank communication.
